Privacy Policy — Elemensys GST Calculator

Effective Date: November 14, 2026
Developer: Elemensys
Contact Email: teamelemensys@gmail.com

1. Overview

Elemensys provides Elemensys GST Calculator as an offline utility application for calculating Goods and Services Tax (GST) with step-by-step breakdowns and generating PDF calculation reports.

2. Information Collection and Use

Elemensys GST Calculator does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, financial calculations, or device identifiers.

  • All GST inputs, saved calculation history, and optional PDF business header fields (Business Name, GSTIN, Address) remain stored exclusively on your device.
  • The app operates completely offline with no account sign-in, no analytics trackers, and no advertising SDKs.

3. PDF Export & File Access

When you export a PDF report, saving uses the Android Storage Access Framework (system file picker) and sharing uses a temporary Android FileProvider URI. The app does not request broad storage permissions.

4. Third-Party Services

No third-party advertising networks or tracking SDKs are integrated into this application.

5. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries, email teamelemensys@gmail.com.
