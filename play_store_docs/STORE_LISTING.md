## Full Description (4000 characters max)
Elemensys GST Calculator is a clean, accurate Goods and Services Tax (GST) calculation tool built for business owners, accountants, freelancers, retailers, and students.

Key Features:
• All GST Percentage Slabs: Instant one-tap calculation for 0%, 0.25%, 1%, 3%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%, and 40%, plus Custom GST % and optional Compensation Cess %.
• Exclusive & Inclusive Modes: Add GST to a net base amount or reverse-calculate the base price from a GST-inclusive total.
• Intra-State & Inter-State Breakdown: Automatic split between CGST + SGST (half rate each) or integrated IGST.
• Step-by-Step Math Breakdown: Every calculation shows numbered steps with exact formulas and substituted values so you can verify and audit every figure.
• All-Slabs Quick Comparison: Compare how any entered amount calculates across every standard GST slab from 0% to 40% at a glance.
• Export PDF Reports: Generate clean, formatted A4 PDF tax calculation reports with optional Business Name, GSTIN, Party Name, and HSN/SAC code. Switch between 10 currency symbols (INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SGD, AUD, CAD, JPY, SAR), save directly to your device, or share instantly.
• Updated HSN/SAC & Product GST Directory: Search recent GST Council updated HSN & SAC codes with applicable rates and one-tap rate application.
• Basic Arithmetic Calculator: Evaluate expressions with parentheses, percentage math, live results, history tape, and one-tap transfer into the GST calculator.
• All-in-One Unit Converter: Instant conversion across 11 categories (Length, Weight, Area, Volume, Temperature, Speed, Time, Data Storage, Pressure, Energy, and Number Base).
• Quantity & Discount Support: Compute multi-unit totals and pre-tax discounts accurately using decimal-precision financial arithmetic.
• Saved Calculation History: Store recent calculations locally on your device and re-export PDF reports anytime.

Offline & Privacy-First:
Elemensys GST Calculator works completely offline without requiring sign-up, tracking, or internet permissions.
